Our Story

Built in a real den (well, a garage).

The Bears Den started as a hobby table at a local market — a few T-shirts, a handful of trinkets, and a lot of encouragement from friends. It grew into something we're proud of.

Everything we carry is chosen or made with the same idea: useful, well-made, and a little bit fun.

  • Small-batch and locally sourced where we can
  • Orders packed by hand, usually within 1–2 days
  • Free local pickup available
